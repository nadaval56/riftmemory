66 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                              Observations of intraclast breccia layers in the Dead Sea basin, formerly termed
67 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         “mixed layers,” provide an exceptionally long and detailed record of past earthquakes
68 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         and define a frontier of paleoseismic research. Multiple studies of these seismites have
69 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         advanced our understanding of the earthquake history of the Dead Sea and of the
70 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         processes that form the intraclast breccias. In this paper, we describe a systematic
71 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         study of intraclast breccia layers in laminated sequences.
72 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                              The relationship of intraclast breccia layers to intraformational fault scarps has
73 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         motivated the investigation of these seismites. Geophysical evidence shows that the
74 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         faults extend into the subsurface, supporting their potential association with strong
75 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         earthquakes.
76 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                              We define field criteria for the recognition of intraclast breccias, focusing on fea-
77 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         tures diagnostic of a seismic origin. The field criteria stem from our understanding of the
78 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         mechanisms of breccia formation, which include ground acceleration, shearing, lique-
79 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         faction, water escape, fluidization, and resuspension of the originally laminated mud.
80 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                              Comparison between a dated record of breccia layer and the record of historical
81 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         earthquakes provides an independent test for a seismic origin. The historical dating
82 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         is significantly more precise and accurate than the radiocarbon dating of breccia lay-
83 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         ers. Yet, assuming that the lamination of the sediments shows an annual cycle, the
84 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         precision of counting laminae may approach the precision of the historical record. A
85 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         similar accuracy is then expected for the intervals between earthquakes. We review
86 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         our work based on counting laminae representing the historical period, mutually cor-
87 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         roborating the seismic origin and the annual lamination.
88 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                              The correlation of documented historical earthquakes with individual breccia
89 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         layers provides quantitative estimates for the threshold of ground motion for breccia
90 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         formation in terms of earthquake magnitude and epicentral distance.

92 [עמ׳ PDF 2]:                         has underscored cases in which a breccia layer represents a pair of earthquakes. We
