84 [עמ׳ PDF 1]: H-Judaic is greatly saddened to learn of the passing, at age 96, of Professor Mordechai Eliav (1920-2016), emeritus professor of Jewish History at Bar Ilan University.  Professor Eliav wrote and edited dozens of books and articles, in Hebrew German and English, dealing with such subjects as Jewish Education in Germany During the Haskalah and Emancipation, the Letters of Esriel Hildesheimer, the history of the Yishuv, Zionism, and the history of the Shoah.  His Hebrew volume, Ani Maamin, a collection of personal testimonials of religious Jews who passed through the Shoah, went through multiple editions in Israel.  Eliav was known for his mastery of primary sources, and his collections of those sources have been of inestimable value to scholars.  He helped to shape the Jewish history program at Bar Ilan, and continued his own scholarly work almost to the end of his life.  We extend deepest condolences to his family.
