180 [עמ׳ PDF 1]: Dr. Motti Zohar is a senior lecturer in the department of Geography and Environmental Studies at the University of Haifa. He is a physical geographer studying natural hazards such as earthquakes, tsunamis and floods. He also develops themes related to Environmental History, in particular the interplay between the environment and human societies and how extreme events affected civilizations in historical times. His other interests are Geographic Information Systems (GIS), spatial models, mapping and cartography.
