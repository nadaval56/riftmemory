  is not exactly a Monarchianist, but an Adoptianist. Benoît Jeanjean shows
  how Jerome presents Photinus, Paulus of Samosate and Sabellius in his book :
  Benoît Jeanjean, "Saint Jérôme et l'hérésie”,
  Paris, Etudes Augustiniennes, 1999, p. 168-177.
