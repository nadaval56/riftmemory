Volume XIV of Charles Haddon Spurgeon's (1834-1892) published sermons. This volume contains numbers 788 through 847 of Rev. Spurgeon's published sermons.\u00a0

The Rev. Charles Haddon Spurgeon (1834 - 1892) is known as \u201cThe Prince of Preachers.\u201d He was the pastor of the New Park Street Chapel in London (later known as the Metropolitan Tabernacle) for 38 years. He was enormously popular as a preacher, and frequently drew more than 10,000 hearers on Sunday Mornings. His sermons were published weekly, and were so popular, that they continued to be published weekly for 25 years after his death. This book is the fourteenth volume (sermon numbers 788 through 847) of these sermons.\u00a0

This is Volume 14 of a total 63 volumes of Rev. Spurgeon\u2019s published sermons.\n\n
